OUR STORY

From Training Programs to Campus Ecosystems

Enbott is part of GT Technologies’ long-term vision to create sustainable, industry-ready talent pipelines in partnership with academia.

How It Started

LEGACY
GT Technologies (formerly HCL Infosystems LLC)
After decades of working with enterprises and technology ecosystems, GT Technologies identified a persistent gap: graduates needed significant re-training before deployment.
CAMPUS FOCUS
Bringing Industry Inside the Campus
Instead of training graduates after they join companies, we began designing programs that could be delivered while students were still in college.
PARTNERSHIPS
CodeChef, Pearson & International Collaborations
We formalized partnerships with global platforms to ensure every student trained through Enbott meets internationally benchmarked standards.

Where We’re Going

The vision is simple: every campus that partners with Enbott should be known for producing graduates who can contribute from Day 1 – whether in a startup, MNC, or their own entrepreneurial venture.

  • Expanding CoEs across Tier 1, 2 and 3 cities
  • Deepening collaboration with global content and assessment partners
  • Creating structured talent pipelines for hiring partners
  • Enabling international exposure and mobility for high-performing students
Our long-term commitment: move from “training as an activity” to “career readiness as a campus culture” across partner institutions.

About Shahul

Shahul Hameed SS is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Enbott, where he leads strategy, business development, and global expansion initiatives. With a strong track record of driving growth, Shahul has steered Enbott toward new markets, including recent engagements in the UAE and broader MEA regions, fostering strategic partnerships and innovative technology collaborations.

Under his leadership, Enbott has focused on delivering future-ready digital solutions across education and enterprise segments, emphasizing scalable IT infrastructure and quality service delivery. Shahul’s vision centers on building an organization that partners with institutions for long-term digital transformation.

Shahul is an advocate for skills development and talent readiness, often speaking on the importance of career training, and creating pathways for emerging professionals. His active engagement with tech communities and partners reflects his commitment to innovation and workforce empowerment.

Shahul’s career blends strong business acumen with strategic leadership, positioning Enbott as a trusted partner in technology-driven growth and capability building. Prior to his entreprenuerial start with Enbott. Shahul worked with HCL and TVS.

Shahul played a pivotal role in establishing and scaling HCL’s operations in the region. Over six years, he contributed to business enablement, operational setup, and ecosystem development, helping position Madurai as a growing technology delivery hub within HCL’s global footprint.

At TVS Group, Shahul worked on business planning and dealership operations, gaining hands-on exposure to large-scale distribution networks, structured business processes, and performance-driven operational models within one of India’s most respected conglomerates.
